# Unit style test, round 2: toy workshop machines (vector v2)

Round 1 (bell, horn, plus-coil icons) was rejected as "extremely simple, way off, our theme is MACHINES". This round draws each family as a small **machine** at middle detail, in the chunky glossy toy style of the red cannon with a star in the painted road map mock (`road-map-painted-v2/normal_try1.png`). Nothing is wired into `src/`.

Generated by `tools/render-unit-style-test-v2.mjs` (SVG -> PNG with sharp, free): `node tools/render-unit-style-test-v2.mjs`.

## Contents
- `svg/<family>_r<rank>.svg` - source art (256x256 viewBox)
- `png/<family>_r<rank>_128.png`, `_256.png` - transparent PNGs
- `sheet_dark.png`, `sheet_light.png` - contact sheets (rows Cannon / Coil / Bell, columns rank 1 / 4 / 8)
- `sheet_grey_dark.png`, `sheet_grey_light.png` - greyscale
- `sheet_48_*.png` - same at 48 px (phone size), colour and greyscale

## The three machines (chosen from UNIT_ROSTER_AUDIT: confusion pairs Cannon/Nail Gun/Rivet Gun, Coil/Capacitor/Dynamo, Bell/Horn)

| Family | Dominant colour | Silhouette | Parts (7 or fewer) |

| **Coil** (Cross) | cyan, copper trim | tall narrow tower with a ball on top, small cart base | 1 base block, 2 two small wheels, 3 tapered tower, 4 two copper winding rings, 5 top ball, 6 bolt badge |
| **Bell** (Row) | yellow, brown wood frame, red trim | square-shouldered A-frame with a dome inside, on a skid | 1 skid base, 2 two small wheels, 3 A-frame posts and crossbeam, 4 bell dome, 5 red band, 6 clapper |

Shared style: warm dark brown outline (#33180f, one weight), top-left light (gradient light/mid/dark per material plus one white gloss shape), a soft ground shadow ellipse, dark purple wheels with a family-trim hub.

## Rank (identical for every family, no parts added)
- Size step: rank 1 = 0.68, rank 4 = 0.82, rank 8 = 0.96 of the frame.
- Pips: one dot per rank under the machine (1 / 4 / 8, two rows of four for rank 5-8).
- Glow ring in the family's light colour: faint at rank 1, medium at 4, strong with a soft halo at 8.
- Family hue never changes with rank.

## Self-review (art-director rubric, quick)
- Greyscale: the three silhouettes stay distinct (low wide diagonal / tall tower / boxy arch). Cannon vs Bell grey values are close; the silhouette carries it.
- 48 px: shape reads; the star and bolt are small but the outline and ring still separate families. Rank 1 vs 4 at 48 px relies on pips + ring.
- Weakness: the Coil's two rings are the only mid-detail; gold hubs on wheels all share one look across families (intended family resemblance).

## Extending to the full 9-family roster
Per family add one entry to `FAMILIES` in the script: `pal` (body and trim gradient light/mid/dark), `glow`, `shadow` ellipse and `parts` (back to front, helpers `wheel`, `star`, `gloss`, `st` for outlines). Rules: one dominant colour, one signature silhouette, 5-7 parts, wheels or a base, a trim colour, a badge. Planned from the audit:
