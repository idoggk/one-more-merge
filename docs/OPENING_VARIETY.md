## Summary (what the numbers say)

1. **The owner is right.** All 77 non-teach levels start from the same 8 parts in the same 8 cells (4 shooters, 2 coils, 2 bells); only the rank (1, 2 or 3) and a few extras differ. Counting ranks, there are 4 distinct cores (rank 1/2/3 and the rocket variant at L6). There are **33 distinct full boards** in 77 levels, but 24 of them belong to a single level: those are the 8 boss/mini-boss levels (their second PAIR8 set is placed on seeded empty cells) plus a few extra/modifier levels. The other 53 levels fall into 9 shared boards. **One board (plain rank 2) is shared by 19 levels (25%)**; the plain rank-1 board by 9 (L4-5, 7, 9, 11, 13-15, 19) and rank-3 by 9.
2. **Move 1 is always the same shape.** Every plain level offers 8 mergeable pairs, all 8 wake a neighbour ("live"), and the best first cascade is 5 machines (6 with a magnet/fan/battery extra). Only boss-type levels (16 parts, 40 pairs) differ. Rank mass is 8 / 16 / 32 for rank 1 / 2 / 3, double on boss levels. The best first-chain move is the same `cannon (1,0) -> (4,1)` in 49 of 77 levels.
3. **Per chapter 4-7 distinct boards in 7-10 levels**, and that count is flattered by the boss/mini-boss (always unique). The ordinary levels of a chapter are mostly 1 or 2 boards (e.g. chapter 8: 6 of 10 levels are the identical plain rank-2 board).
4. **A good player plays the same opening everywhere.** The greedy player's first 3 moves are `cannon pair -> bell pair -> second cannon pair` ((1,0)>(4,1); (1,3)>(3,2); (3,4)>(4,2)) in 25 of 77 levels (32%) for the chain-seeker and 28 of 77 (36%) for the damage-seeker; the top 3 openings (one per starting rank) cover 48 of 77 levels (chain) / 53 of 77 (damage), and only 26 / 24 different 3-move openings exist at all (20 ignoring rank, 18 ignoring cells), most of the rare ones on boss or extra levels.
5. **Proposal (section 5, not implemented):** a seeded overlay that re-places the 8 starter parts on one of 4 layouts per ordinary level. Evaluated in the tool: distinct boards **33 -> 50 overall, 45 -> 66 summed over chapters (4-7 -> 6-10 per chapter)**, largest identical-board group **19 -> 6** levels, distinct greedy 3-move openings **26 -> 37**, most common opening 25 -> 8 levels; rank mass and part count unchanged for all 77 levels; >= 5 live pairs at move 1 everywhere (needs >= 2). Cost: layouts B/C open weaker (best chain 4 vs 5, damage x0.75) and D much weaker (chain 3, damage x0.43), so the HP/star lines (tuned on layout A) need a refit pass with `tools/level-report.ts` before shipping.

### 5c. The deal (which level gets which layout)

Rule: per chapter, walk the levels in order with a deck `shuffle([0,1,2,3,0,1,2,3])` drawn from `new Rng(SALT ^ chapter)` (refilled when empty). Each ordinary level takes the first card in the deck that differs from the previous level's layout, so every layout is used 2-3 times per chapter and two neighbouring levels never share one. Boss levels, mini-bosses and the scripted teach levels L1-L3 keep layout A (the boss second PAIR8 set already makes them unique); they count as "previous = A" for the next level. In the real build the deal would be `layoutFor(def)` = a pure function of `def.level` (not of `def.seed`, so it can be hand-checked), applied inside newLevel just before `start_extra`.
